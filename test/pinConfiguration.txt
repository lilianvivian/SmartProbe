# Notes

- OLED and BME688 share the same I²C bus.
- SIM800L communicates over UART.
- Pump and fan are enabled only during **Critical** Guardian status.
- LEDs indicate:
  - Green → Safe
  - Yellow → Warning
  - Red → Critical
- Buzzer sounds according to Guardian status.
- Battery voltage is monitored by the ESP32 ADC.
